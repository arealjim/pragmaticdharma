## Gotcha — a redeploy brings a site back

The sub-project repos still declare their custom domain in `wrangler.toml`, so
`wrangler deploy` from one of them re-attaches the domain and makes it public
again. Don't deploy them unless you mean to restore them.

## Restoring one

1. Deploy from its repo (re-attaches the custom domain), or re-add the domain via
   `POST /accounts/<acct>/workers/domains` (hostname, service, zone_id).
2. Set its `status` back to `'live'` in `projects.config.mjs`, add its row to
   `EXPECTED_CARDS` in `test/registry-render.test.mjs`, `npm test`, `npm run deploy`.
